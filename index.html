About the game

إلعبها صح (Elaabha Sah) is a bluffing card game for 2 to 10 players, each on their own phone. Everyone holds face-down character cards, which are their lives, and collects coins. On your turn you claim a character and use its power, whether you really hold that card or not. Anyone who thinks you're lying can challenge you. If you were telling the truth, they lose a card. If you were bluffing, you do. The last player with a card left wins.

Online play only

Over Wi-Fi · عبر الواي فاي

Every player plays on their own phone, connected to the internet over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Phones don't need to be on the same network or in the same place, so friends can play together from different cities or countries. There is no offline or single-phone mode.

2 to 10 players · من ٢ إلى ١٠ لاعبين

A room holds up to 10 players. You need at least 2 to start. Team mode needs at least 6 players.

Starting a game

  1. Every player installs the game and makes sure their phone is connected to the internet.
  2. One player taps Start Game (ابدأ لعبة), types a name and taps Create Room (إنشاء غرفة). The game shows an 8-digit room code.
  3. The other players tap Join Game (انضم للعبة), type their names and enter the room code.
  4. In the room, choose the settings (below). Each player then taps Ready. When everyone is ready, a 3-second countdown starts the game.

Room settings

Turn timer

Off, 30 seconds, 1 minute or 1.5 minutes per turn.

Coup cost

10, 12 or 15 coins.

Cards per player

2 or 3 lives each.

Additional characters Premium

Add up to 3 of the 14 premium characters, and choose how many copies of each card are in the deck.

Teams

With 6 or more players: split into 2 to 5 teams and give them any names you like.

How to play

  1. Everyone starts with 2 coins and 2 or 3 face-down cards. Your cards are your lives: lose them all and you're out. In a 2-player game, the starting player begins with only 1 coin.
  2. On your turn you take exactly one action: a general action, or the action of a character.
  3. You may claim any character, even one you don't hold. Bluffing is the game! Just don't get caught. A few characters can't be bluffed: they only work if you really hold them.
  4. Other players can challenge your claim. If you were honest, you reveal the card, shuffle it back and draw a new one, and the challenger loses a card. If you were bluffing, you lose a card and the action fails (any coins you paid come back).
  5. Some actions can be blocked by claiming a character, and a block can be challenged too.
  6. When you lose a card, you choose which one to reveal. Revealed cards stay face-up for everyone. The last player with a card left wins. In team mode, the last team with a surviving member wins.

Who can respond

For an action without a target, such as Tax, Exchange or Foreign Aid, every other player may challenge or allow it. For an action aimed at a player, only that player can challenge, allow or block it, and only the player who acted can challenge the block. Other players can't step in, not even teammates.

General actions (anyone can take them)

  • Income: take 1 coin. Can't be blocked.
  • Foreign Aid: take 2 coins. Anyone claiming the King can block it.
  • Coup: pay the Coup cost (10, 12 or 15) to make a player lose a card. It can't be blocked, challenged or redirected. If you start your turn with 3 coins more than the Coup cost (13 when a Coup costs 10), you must Coup.

Timer

If your turn runs out, you automatically take Income (or Coup, if you must). If a response runs out, it counts as Allow. If picking a card runs out, one is picked at random.

The cards

The game's How to Play (طريقة اللعب) screen lists every card, and during a game you can tap any visible card to read what it does.

The five basic cards (always in the game)

King · القائد

Tax: take 3 coins from the treasury. Blocks Foreign Aid.

Thief · السارقة

Steal: take 2 coins from another player. Blocks stealing.

Assassin · القاتل

Assassinate: pay 3 coins to make a player lose a card.

Investigator · المحقق

No action of his own, but blocks assassination.

Ambassador · السفير

Exchange: draw 2 cards from the deck and choose what to keep. Blocks stealing.

The 14 premium characters

With Premium, up to 3 of these can join a game on top of the basic cards. Cards marked Real card only can't be bluffed, so they can't be challenged either.

Bodyguard · الحارس Real card only

Stops a targeted action against you, except a Coup or a secret Pickpocket. Once per game, use your turn to guard yourself or a teammate until your next turn, even from a Coup.

Broker · السمسار

A player swaps one of their cards, picked at random, with the deck.

Captain · القبطان

Raid: pay 3 coins and the target loses a card, unless they pay 3 coins to escape.

Dealer · الموزّعة

Give a player one of your cards. They give you one of theirs back, both unseen.

Hacker · الهاكر

Pay 4 coins to lock a character for a player's next turn, for real or as a bluff.

Merchant · التاجر Real card only

Once you've lost a card, pay 10 coins to get one back (once per game). Blocks the Hacker and the Captain's raid.

Negotiator · المفاوض

Offer a player 3 coins to see one of their cards. They can accept, turn it down or challenge.

Pickpocket · النشّال Real card only

Secretly take 1 coin from another player. Everyone else sees you take Income.

Prey · الفريسة Real card only

Lose it to an attack or a challenge, and whoever attacked or accused you reveals all their cards.

Puppeteer · محرّك الدمى

Redirect an action aimed at you to another valid target. Doesn't work on a Coup.

Saboteur · المخرّب

Destroy 3 of another player's coins. Only 3 sabotages per game, shared by everyone.

The Shadow · الظل Real card only

Copy the last action another player took, paying what it costs.

Spy · الجاسوس Real card only

Secretly look at one of a player's cards, picked at random.

Viper · الأفعى Real card only

On their next turn, a player can only use cards they really hold. No bluffing.

Teams

With 6 or more players you can turn on teams. Everyone joins a team, you can't attack your own teammates, and the team with the last surviving member wins.

Common questions

How many players do we need?

At least 2 and up to 10, each on their own phone. Team mode needs at least 6 players.

Does the game need an internet connection?

Yes. Every player needs their own phone connected to the internet. Wi-Fi and mobile data both work, and you don't need to be on the same network or in the same place. The game has no offline mode.

My friends can't join my room ("Room not found").

  • Check that they entered all 8 digits of the room code.
  • Make sure every phone is connected to the internet.
  • Make sure the room is still open. A room closes automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left it. If it closed, create a new one.
  • Make sure everyone has the latest version of the game. The game asks you to update when a newer version is needed.

The room refused to let me join.

Either the room is full (10 players) or its game has already started. If you were in that game, join with the same room code and the same name to get your seat back. Otherwise, wait for the game to end or create a new room.

Someone lost their connection or left during a game.

The game stops and waits for them. To come back, they tap Join Game (انضم للعبة) and enter the same room code and the same name. If the app was closed, the home screen shows a Rejoin button for that game for up to 3 hours. The player who runs the room can also take everyone back to the setup screen, and can remove an offline player from the room.

Does everyone in the room need Premium?

No. If one player in the room owns Premium, everyone in that room can play with the premium characters. Without Premium, the player who runs the room can watch a short ad to open the premium characters for the next game. If the Premium owner leaves, the next game goes back to the basic cards.

I bought Premium but it isn't on.

Connect to the internet, open the Premium (مميز) screen and tap Confirm purchase (تأكيد الشراء) or Restore purchase (استعادة الشراء). This checks your purchase with Google Play or the App Store again and turns Premium on. Do the same after reinstalling the game or moving to a new phone, signed in with the same Google account or Apple ID you bought with.

Do I need to pay again on a new phone?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase linked to your Google account or Apple ID. Install the game, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchase.

Are our games or scores saved?

No. There are no accounts, profiles or leaderboards. A game only lasts while its room is open, and rooms are deleted from our server automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left.

Can I play in English?

Yes. The game is in Arabic and English. Change the language from Settings (الإعدادات) → Language (اللغة).

I found a bug.

Email us with what happened, how many players were in the room, and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot. Bug reports are really useful and we fix them quickly.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information إلعبها صح (Elaabha Sah) ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login or sign-up of any kind. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the App does not access them.

Player Names and Game Data

To play, you type a name of up to 10 characters. It is used only to show other players who is who in the room:

  • When you create or join a room, the App sends the following to our game server so the game can run: the name you enter, the room code, your in-game actions (such as the actions you take, your challenges and blocks, and the cards you choose), and whether your phone owns Premium (yes or no), so the room knows whether premium characters are allowed.
  • The server gives your seat a random player ID and a random key, so that you can get back into the same seat if your connection drops. They are not linked to your identity.
  • The server shares your name and the public state of the game (such as coins, revealed cards and actions taken) with the other players in the same room, as the game requires. Your face-down cards are never sent to other players' phones.
  • Room data is kept on the server only while the room is in use, and is deleted automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left the room.
  • Other players in your room can see the name you enter. You are free to use a nickname.

Our Game Server

Online rooms run on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. All connections to it are encrypted. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to deliver the connection, and keeps short technical logs (such as the time of a request and basic connection details) for a few days, which we use only to keep the service working and fix problems. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not sell or share it.

Google Firebase

The App uses Google Firebase for two things:

  • Update check: when the App starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version of the App is required. Firebase uses a random installation identifier and basic information (such as the App version and platform) for this.
  • Purchase check: when you buy or restore Premium, the purchase receipt issued by the store is checked by our service on Firebase, as described under In-App Purchases below.

Firebase is operated by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your own device:

  • The name you last played with, so you don't have to type it again.
  • Your language choice (Arabic or English).
  • The code of the last room you were in, with its player ID and key, so you can rejoin an unfinished game within 3 hours.
  • Whether you own Premium, so it stays on.
  • Your ad consent choices, where they apply.

This information is never sent to us, except the name, room code, player ID and key when you join or rejoin a room, as described above. All of it is removed if you uninstall the App.

No banner ads are shown during a game. Buying Premium removes all ads.

AdMob may also show ads from its partner ad networks, such as Unity Ads. To serve and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK and its partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, basic device information, and how you interact with ads. We do not control this data collection directly. It is handled by those companies under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review: Google at policies.google.com/privacy (and how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and Unity at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from Settings → Privacy settings in the App. Players in US states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of their information from the same place. You can also limit ad personalization at any time from your device's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on iOS).

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase, Premium, which removes all ads and unlocks the 14 premium characters for you and everyone in the rooms you play in. The free version of the game is fully playable without buying anything.

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). To make sure a purchase is genuine, the App sends the purchase receipt issued by the store to our purchase-check service (Google Cloud Functions, through Firebase), which asks Google Play or Apple to confirm it. The receipt contains no payment details. We only keep a short log of which product was checked and whether it was confirmed. Restoring a purchase works the same way.
